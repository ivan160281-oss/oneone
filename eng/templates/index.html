WiFi-only locate (no GPS)

Paste the SSIDs currently visible on your device (comma-separated) to get a rough position estimate based on previously uploaded data. Accuracy depends entirely on how much matching data has been collected, and on how unique those SSIDs are (see note below).

Note on accuracy: WiFi networks are matched by BSSID (the unique MAC address of each access point) whenever it's available, which is much more precise than matching by name alone. There's no existing public database mapping any of these identifiers to real-world coordinates, so all positioning here comes purely from your own accumulated uploads. Check out Algorithm Lab for a higher-confidence, "stable points only" version of this database.
