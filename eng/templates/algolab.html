Algorithm Lab: stable point detection

The regular network database averages every sighting of a network together, whether it's been seen once or twenty times. This algorithm instead looks for WiFi networks / BLE devices that reappear at essentially the same place across multiple different uploaded sessions - the more independent re-confirmations, and the tighter the cluster of positions, the higher its stability score. This builds a separate, prioritized "stable points" database you can use instead of the regular one on the Point Lab page. Runs only when you trigger it - not part of the daily automatic job.
